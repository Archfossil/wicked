# 09 — Tricks

What is a villain without a bag of tricks?

## Rules (decided)

- **Rule:** each player starts with **3 Tricks**, each picked by drawing 2 and keeping 1 (answers Q-60).
- **Rule:** Tricks are **hard to come by**. You get more from:
  - certain Rooms,
  - certain abilities,
  - trading in certain Hero souls.
- **Rule:** Tricks are played for many kinds of effects:
  - hinder Heroes,
  - hinder other villains,
  - boost your own side,
  - build or upgrade rooms,
  - destroy or limit others.

- **Rule (answers Q-63):** each Trick **prints the phase(s)** it can be played in (e.g. **Build Phase Tricks**). There are **no reactions**: a Trick can't be played outside its printed phases. See [Round Flow](02-round-flow.md).
- **Rule (answers Q-65):** there's **no Trick market**. Whenever something gives you a Trick, **draw 2 from the Trick deck and keep 1**; the other is discarded.
- **Rule:** Spells recharge in **Upkeep**.
- **Rule (answers Q-64):** each Trick prints its own **cost to play**, set by how strong it is: **free**, **Coins**, or **souls**. Mage overflow adds +1 Coin to every Trick played.
- **Rule (answers Q-62):** **hand limit 7** (Rooms and Tricks together). At the **end of the End Phase**, discard down to 7.

## Sub-types

| Sub-type | Status | Behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Spell** | **Rule** | Not discarded after use. It becomes **Exhausted** for its **printed recharge time** (1, 2 or 3 Upkeeps). |
| Construction | Idea | Builds or upgrades Rooms or Terrain. |
| Deceit | Idea | Hits other villains: steal, lure Heroes away. **Rule:** some Tricks work on the Objective Market: peek at the next Objectives, throw a face-up one out, or claim a "have it" Objective before the End Phase check. |
| Empower | Idea | Boosts your Villain, Champions or Monsters. |
| Command | Idea | Controls Heroes or Monsters, e.g. pick a Hero's move direction instead of rolling for it. |

Anything that isn't a Spell is assumed to be **discarded after one use**.
