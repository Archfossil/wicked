# 01 — Setup

These steps run in order before round 1.

## 1. Villain draft

- **Rule:** each player draws **5** Villain cards.
- **Rule:** pick 1 and pass the other **4 to the left**.
- **Rule:** pick 1 from the 4 you were handed and pass the other **3** to the left.
- **Rule:** pick 1 from the 3 you were handed. Remove the 2 leftovers from the game.
- Each player ends with **3 Villains**. One runs the dungeon and the others are reserves. See [Villains](04-villains.md).

```
draw 5 → keep 1, pass 4 ← → keep 1, pass 3 ← → keep 1, discard 2
```

- **Rule (answers Q-10/Q-11):** each player **secretly chooses** a starting Villain, then everyone reveals at once. The other 2 stay **face-down** as reserves. When a Villain is vanquished, you **choose** which reserve steps in and reveal it then.

## 2. Lay out the markets

- Shuffle the Dungeon deck and fill the **Dungeon Market**.
- Shuffle the Terrain and Monster decks and fill the **Terrain Market** with Terrain + Monster pairs.
- Shuffle the Champion and Treasure decks and fill the **Champion Market** (3) and **Treasure Market** (players + 2).
- How markets work is in [Markets](03-markets.md).
- **Rule:** the Dungeon and Terrain markets each have **players + 2** slots. The **Champion market** has **3** slots.

## 3. Opening room draft

- **Rule:** taking turns, each player either takes a Room from the **Dungeon Market** or draws one blind off the **top of the Dungeon deck**. Keep going until every player holds **5 Room cards**.
- **Rule:** when a card comes out of the market, it refills by the normal market rule (shift left, new card on the right).
- **Rule (answers Q-03):** the draft is a **snake**: one pick at a time from player 1 to N, then N back to 1, until everyone has 5. Player 1 is chosen **at random**, and is also the **first player in round 1**.
- **Rule (answers Q-40):** these 5 Rooms go into your **hand**. Your dungeon starts **empty**, and you build Rooms over time in Build Phases.

## 4. Starting Tricks

- **Rule:** each player starts with **3 Trick cards**.
- **Rule (answers Q-60):** the same way as gaining a Trick later: **draw 2, keep 1**, three times.

## 5. Other setup (open)

- **Rule:** deal each player **5 objectives**, and each player **chooses 1 to reveal** (answers Q-77). See [Objectives](05-objectives-and-scoring.md).
- **Rule:** each player starts with **3 Coins**. Starting treasure and souls: assumed none.
- **Rule (answers Q-50):** reveal **1 Hero per player** into the Town, so there's something to lure in round 1. The Town has **no cap**; overflow penalties are the only limit.
- Every Overworld starts as an empty 4×4 wasteland (Rule). See [Overworld](07-overworld.md).
