# 06 — Dungeon & Champions

## Rooms

- **Rule:** Rooms come from the Dungeon deck through the [Dungeon Market](03-markets.md). In the Town Phase a player can take 1 Room instead of buying a Champion or drafting Terrain.
- **Rule:** each player starts with 5 Room cards **in hand** from the opening draft. Dungeons start **empty** and get built over time (answers Q-40).
- **Rule:** Heroes who survive the [Overworld](07-overworld.md) reach the **dungeon entrance**, then go through the rooms **one space per round**, then fight the Villain. An **empty** space still takes a round to cross.
- **Idea:** Tricks can build, upgrade, destroy or limit rooms.
- **Idea:** some rooms give extra Tricks.

### Dungeon layout: spaces and stacks

- **Rule:** a dungeon is made of **spaces**. In the Build Phase a Room can go into an **empty space** or **on top of an existing Room**, which makes a **stack**.
- **Rule:** you can place **1 Room or 1 Champion** per Build Phase.
- **Rule:** in the Build Phase you can **destroy the top Room** of a stack.
- **Rule:** stacks get stronger as they grow. The **top Room** gives its full effect, and every Room **underneath** it still gives a small **covered bonus** (a passive upgrade printed on the card).
- **Derived:** if you destroy the top Room, the Room below becomes the new top and gives its full effect again.
- **Rule (answers Q-45):** a dungeon has **5 spaces in a line** from the entrance to the Villain, matching the 5 opening Rooms. Dungeons grow **by stacking**, not by adding spaces.
- **Rule:** some Rooms are **empowered while a Minion is in them**. See [Minions](13-minions.md).
- **Rule:** Rooms with creatures in them fight at **Monster rank** (see [Combat](02-round-flow.md#combat-dice-pool)).
- **Rule:** **trap Rooms** (no creatures) are also Monster rank, and they **test Cunning**.
- **Rule:** some Rooms **produce Minions** in Upkeep (e.g. a Barracks).
- **Rule (answers Q-49a):** stacks have **no height limit**. A destroyed Room is simply **discarded**, with no refund.
- **Rule (answers Q-41):** each Room prints a **threat**: the stat it tests, its dice and its damage, resolved like a Hazard. Trap Rooms test Cunning. Some Rooms have an **effect** instead of, or as well as, a roll.
- **Rule (answers Q-43):** some Rooms **produce treasure** (e.g. a Vault): they add treasure to your treasury in **Upkeep**, or count as treasure of a type while on top. Their card says which.
- **Rule (answers Q-49b):** a Champion belongs to the **space**, not the Room. When its Room is covered or destroyed, the Champion **moves to whichever Room is now on top**.
- **Open:** what happens to the Champion if the last Room in a space is destroyed? → Q-49c

## Champions

- **Rule:** in the Town Phase a player can **buy a Champion** instead of taking a Room or Terrain. Champions come from a face-up **Champion market of 3**, at each card's **printed price** (about 2 to 5 Coins).
- **Rule (answers Q-42):** each dungeon space holds **1 Champion**. As a Build Phase extra, you can **move a Champion** to another space that has no Champion.
- **Rule (answers Q-42a):** you can hire a Champion into a space that already has one. The **old Champion is discarded**. You can move **only one Champion** per Build Phase.
- **Rule:** in the Build Phase a Champion is placed in a Room. It **occupies the room without replacing it** and gives that room bonuses, making it more dangerous for Heroes.
- **Rule:** a Room with a Champion fights at **Champion rank** (so Common Heroes lose ties there) and rolls **the Champion's dice if they're higher** than the Room's.
- **Rule:** in the Build Phase you can **advance** a Champion by **paying** it. Champions are mercenaries, so their effects grow with their pay.
- **Rule (answers Q-48):** each Champion **prints its own levels** (usually 2–3 above its base), each with a **Coin price** and a **new effect**.
- **Rule (answers Q-47):** pay is **only for advancing**. Once hired, a Champion stays for free.
- **Rule:** covering or destroying the Room doesn't lose the Champion. It guards the new top Room (answers Q-49b).
