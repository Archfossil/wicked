# 04 — Villains

## Villains are your lives

- **Rule:** each player drafts **3 Villains** at setup. See [Setup](01-setup.md).
- **Rule:** one Villain is active at a time and waits at the end of your dungeon.
- **Rule:** you choose your starting Villain in secret. Reserves stay **face-down**, and you choose who steps in when a Villain falls.
- **Rule:** a Hero who gets through your whole dungeon **fights your active Villain**.
- **Rule:** if the Hero wins, that Villain is **vanquished**. One of your other Villains **takes over the dungeon**.
- **Rule:** if your last Villain is vanquished, you are **eliminated** and lose the game.
- **Rule (answers Q-12):** **everything carries over** when a new Villain takes over: dungeon, Overworld, Champions, Minions, Coins, souls, treasury, Tainted Items and hand. Only the Villain card changes (plus the stolen Item). Claimed Objectives are kept.

## What every Villain has (draft)

| Field | Notes |

The Villain fight is **to the death**, one exchange each round. Villains are **Villain rank**, so only Legendary Heroes win ties against them. A winning Hero deals tier damage (1/2/3) + 1 for each extra hit. |

## Resources a villain (player) manages

**Rule:** Coins and Treasure are **separate resources** (answers Q-30). Coins get spent. Treasure sits in your treasury and lures Heroes.

| Resource | Uses |
| --- | --- |
| **Souls** | Harvested from dead Heroes: **Common 1, Paragon 2, Legendary 3**. Track two numbers: **souls you have** (spent on prizes in the End Phase, and some Hero souls can be swapped for Tricks) and **total souls harvested** (never goes down). The total unlocks which Treasures you can make and is the game's tie-break (answers Q-31). |
| **Coins** | Spent: buying Champions, advancing (paying) Champions, and possibly building. **Earned:** 1 each Upkeep, plus loot from defeated Heroes (Common 1, Paragon 2, Legendary 3). You start with **3**. → Q-33b (loot timing) |
| **Treasure** | Kept in your dungeon's **treasury**. The treasure count and treasure types lure Heroes (see [Heroes & Town](08-heroes-and-town.md#luring)). New Treasures unlock as your total souls harvested passes thresholds. Types: Warrior, Mage, Acolyte, Rogue and Gold. Each **Treasure card** prints its own **soul threshold** (total souls harvested needed) and **Coin cost** (answers Q-34). They're bought from the **Treasure Market** in the End Phase (answers Q-34a). |
| **Tainted Items** | Taken from defeated Heroes. You can **sustain 2**, and their villain effects may recharge in Upkeep. Spares can be given away or stored in the treasury as class-specific lures. See [Items](12-items.md). |

## Minions

Villains give orders to **Minions**: non-combatant lackeys who empower Rooms, go to the Town to help lure Heroes, and carry out "Send a Minion to…" powers. See [Minions](13-minions.md).
