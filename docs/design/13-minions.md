# 13 — Minions

Minions are the villain's lackeys. They don't fight. They're **non-combatant resources** that the Villain **orders around**.

> Not the same as **Monsters**. "Monster" is the combat rank of Overworld Monsters and of Rooms with creatures in them. Minions never roll combat dice.

## Rules (decided)

- **Rule:** Minions are **identical tokens** (answers Q-18a).
- **Rule:** Minions **come from certain Rooms**. For example, a *Barracks* gives 1 Minion each Upkeep (answers Q-18b).
- **Rule (answers Q-18b, cap):** you can hold at most **5 Minions**. Only a Room **on top** of its stack produces them; a covered Barracks stops.
- **Rule:** Minions are **non-combatants**. They don't take part in fights.
- **Rule (answers Q-18e):** Minions are **never lost**. Orders and "Send a Minion to…" powers move them around but don't use them up, and Heroes can't kill or capture them.
- **Rule (answers Q-18f):** a Minion with no new order **stays where it is** and keeps doing its job (empowering a Room, tempting in Town) until you re-order it. Town Minions don't come home on their own.
- **Rule (orders, answers Q-18c):** in the **Build Phase**, you give each Minion **one order** for the round. Card powers can say things like *"Send a Minion to do X"*.
- **Rule:** some **Rooms are empowered** while a Minion is in them.
- **Rule (Town, answers Q-18d):** a Minion sent to the **Town** gives your dungeon **+1 tempt** on **one Hero of your choice** in the Lure Phase that round.

## Possible orders

| Order | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Stay in a Room | Empowers that Room if it has a Minion bonus |
| Go to Town | +1 tempt each round it stays there, on a Hero you **choose each Lure Phase** (answers Q-18g). If its last target was lured away, just pick another |
| Spy (via cards) | Learn another player's objective, or force an objective swap or replacement. See [Objectives](05-objectives-and-scoring.md) |
| Card-specific | "Send a Minion to…" effects on Rooms, Champions, Tricks and Items |

## Example cards (illustration only)

- *Room: Barracks.* Upkeep: gain 1 Minion.
- *Room: Torture Chamber.* While a Minion is here, this Room rolls +1 die.
- *Champion: Overseer.* At level 2, each Minion in this Room counts as 1 extra treasure.
